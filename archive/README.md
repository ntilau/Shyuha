# Archived Research

## Why These Documents Are Here

This repository is now the business plan for a geothermal power venture in Rwanda. Two earlier research documents covered subjects outside that scope — solar photovoltaic ROI and municipal water-pumping energy. They are preserved here, unedited, because they remain sound research and may be useful for reference.

They are not part of the business plan and nothing in the plan depends on them.

## Contents

- [SOLAR_ROI_ANALYSIS.md](SOLAR_ROI_ANALYSIS.md) — Return on investment analysis for solar PV systems from 100 kW to 10 MW supplying Kigali City. Retained because its methodology (capacity factor, degradation, payback, sensitivity) is a useful comparison point, and because solar is a plausible complement to distributed geothermal direct use.
- [WATER_PUMPING_ENERGY.md](WATER_PUMPING_ENERGY.md) — Energy required to pump water to Kigali, covering static head, friction losses and annual cost. Retained because municipal water pumping remains a credible future customer for geothermal heat, though it is not part of the current plan.

## Caution When Reusing These Figures

Both documents price electricity at Rwanda's **retail consumer tariff of 182 RWF/kWh (~$0.16 USD/kWh)**, which was the convention in the repository at the time. A power producer does not sell at the retail tariff; it sells at a negotiated generation tariff, which is lower. Any figure in these documents that treats retail tariff as project revenue should not be reused without correction.

The implied exchange rate in these documents is approximately 1,137 RWF/USD. It is never stated explicitly in the original text; see `../DATA_GAPS_AND_ASSUMPTIONS.md` for the plan's stated FX convention.

## Status

Both documents are **superseded and unedited**. They have not been re-dated, re-sourced or corrected, and they retain their original figures — including national statistics that were accurate when written and are now out of date. Do not cite them as current.
