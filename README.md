# Shyuha Project

## Rwanda Energy Sector Research Repository

This repository aggregates research documents, datasets, and findings related to Rwanda's energy sector, with a special focus on geothermal energy potential, exploration, and development. It serves as a centralized knowledge base for researchers, policymakers, and stakeholders interested in Rwanda's sustainable energy transition.

## Contents

- **JICA.md** - Findings from the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) project on Rwanda's Electricity Development Plan
- **MININFRA.md** - Summary of Rwanda's Ministry of Infrastructure (MININFRA) energy sector information, including current energy mix and access statistics
- **SUMMARY.md** - Comprehensive research findings on geothermal energy potential, prospect zones, exploration history, and development plans for Rwanda

## Project Focus

The Shyuha project collects and organizes information about:
- Rwanda's national energy mix (hydropower, solar, methane, peat, biomass) and sector planning
- Geothermal energy prospects in the northwestern volcanic zone (Gisenyi, Karisimbi, Kinigi) and southern rift-fault area (Bugarama)
- Exploration efforts, drilling results, and feasibility studies for geothermal development
- International cooperation projects, particularly JICA-supported initiatives for energy sector planning
- Rwanda's energy access goals (universal access by 2024) and sustainable development strategies
- Climate commitment alignment, including Rwanda's 2030 emissions-reduction pledge
